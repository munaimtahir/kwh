Kwh Logger is a simple, offline electricity meter logbook for homes, offices, or labs that run
one or more meters.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Create one or more meters (e.g. "Home Main", "Clinic Lab").
- Add readings with an optional note whenever you check the meter.
- See each meter's latest reading at a glance, plus history and a trend chart.
- Set a reminder (frequency + time) so you never forget to log a reading; snooze reminders from
  the notification itself.
- Export your readings to CSV to back up or share your data — no cloud account needed.
- Group meters into an "environment" if your household switches load between multiple
  connections to manage billing-slab thresholds; the app tracks which meter is active and
  recommends when to switch to the next one before you cross a threshold.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT
- 100% offline. All data stays on your device — no accounts, no analytics, no network access.
- No ads.
- Designed around slab/tiered electricity billing, where knowing your cycle usage rate before the
  billing cutoff actually matters.

WHO IT'S FOR
Anyone who wants a straightforward, private meter log with gentle reminders — not a smart-meter
integration, just a fast way to record numbers and understand your usage trend.
