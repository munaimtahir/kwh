# Play Console Data Safety form — draft answers

Based on [`privacy-policy.md`](privacy-policy.md): the app has no network access and no backend,
so the entire form should be answered as "no data collected."

- **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** No.
- **Data types collected:** None (no location, personal info, financial info, health, messages,
  photos/videos, files/docs, calendar, contacts, app activity, device/other IDs — nothing).
- **Is all user data encrypted in transit?** N/A — no data leaves the device.
- **Do you provide a way for users to request data deletion?** N/A — nothing is collected;
  uninstalling the app deletes all local data.
- **Data safety practices independently reviewed?** N/A.

Re-verify this against the live Play Console form at submission time, since Google periodically
