# Privacy Policy — Kwh Logger

_Last updated: 2026-08-02_

Kwh Logger ("the app") is developed by Vexel. This policy explains how the app handles your
information.

## Data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no backend server, no
analytics SDK, and no network access. All data you enter — meters, readings, notes, reminder
settings, and environment/switch history — is stored locally on your device using an on-device
database (Room/SQLite) and local preferences storage.

## Data sharing

The app does not share data with any third party, because it does not transmit data anywhere.
CSV export is a user-initiated action that creates a file on your device (or shares it via the
Android share sheet to an app of your choosing) — the app itself does not send this file
anywhere.

## Permissions

The app requests one runtime permission: **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, Android 13+),
used solely to show local reminder notifications you configure. No other permissions are
requested.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data persists only on your device for as long as the app is installed. Uninstalling the app
or clearing its storage from Android Settings permanently deletes all locally stored data. There
is no remote copy to delete because none exists.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not
collect data at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised
"Last updated" date.
